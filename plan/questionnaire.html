Website study · questionnaire

The website, in two parts

Five minutes. Part one: a picture of the home page for ten seconds, then five questions. Part two: the live site for up to two minutes, then seven more. Web version: hannahadamsdesign-spec.github.io/brand-study/site/

Respondent ID ______________   Channel ______________   Device: phone / laptop   Date ____________

Before anything

S1
What best describes your work?
S2
How old are you?
S3
Before today, had you heard of a company called F1R3FLY?

Part one: ten seconds

Show the home-page picture (laptop or phone version to match their device) for ten seconds, then take it away.
A1
What was this page about?
A2
In your own words, what do you think this company does?
A3
Who do you think this is designed for?
A4
What action did the page want you to take? (leave blank if you couldn't tell)
A5
How trustworthy did the page feel?
12345
1 = Not at all5 = Very

Part two: the live site, up to two minutes

Open f1r3fly.io and look around as you normally would. Come back when you are done or when the two minutes are up.
B1
Now that you've looked around: in your own words, what does this company do?
B2
What do they offer? List anything you remember.
B3
Which industry do you think this company is in? Pick one.
Read the list in a different order each time; keep Not sure last.
B4
Which of these words describe this company, based on what you saw? Pick up to three.
B5
How well did the site explain what the company does?
12345
1 = Not at all5 = Completely
B6
Which of these would you do next, if anything?
B7
Was anything unclear or confusing? (optional)
Willing to talk for fifteen minutes? Email (optional), used once, deleted within thirty days.
