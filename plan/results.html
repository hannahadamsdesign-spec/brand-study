How we read the results

Every answer lands as one row in a shared Google Sheet. One tab per study. These are the numbers we pull from it, per name, and what each one means.

What would change your mind

Decision 4 on the plan page asks each of Greg, Nicholas and Hannah for one sentence, written before any data exists. Those sentences are the first thing the readout quotes, next to the number that answers them. If nobody writes one, the study cannot change anything and we should not run it.

What this cannot tell us

Which name will build a brand. Research rules names out and prices their costs; it does not pick winners. It also says nothing about trademark, domains, or search collisions; those run on their own track and get read next to this, not instead of it.
