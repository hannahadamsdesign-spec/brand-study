The paid panel, in numbers

Prolific's recommended rate is $12 an hour. A three-minute study pays each person about $0.60; Prolific adds a 42.9 percent fee for companies, so a complete answer costs about $0.86. Two hundred answers is about $170, plus small payments to people screened out. The next-day recall question is a second short study at the same rate. Check that Prolific's built-in screener covers "software developer" before budgeting a custom one.

The caps

Nicholas and Patrick get stopped by strangers of every age asking what the cap says. That is attention, not comprehension, and it is the one piece of real-world data we have. If anyone wants to turn it into something countable: three questions, asked on the spot, "what do you think it says, what do you think it is, would you look it up." Optional.

Invite copy, same everywhere

"Two-minute study on a company name. You'll see one name and answer a few questions. No right answers." Nothing about F1R3FLY, nothing about the debate. For the website study: "Five-minute study about a company website. No right answers."
